        Find your ideal carb level

        Your body is unique

        The foods and carb amounts that work for one person may not work the same way for another. Finding your ideal carb level starts with learning how your body responds to carbs.

        Carbs affect people differently

        Even with the same carbs, one person's blood sugar may stay fairly steady while another's may rise quickly. Your response is shaped by many factors, including activity, sleep, stress, medications, insulin sensitivity, and the rest of the meal.

        You can't tell by looking

        You can't tell how someone responds to carbs just by looking at them. That's why your own feedback matters more than a one-size-fits-all carb target.

        Ketones signal fat-burning

        Ketones are one type of feedback. When carbs stay low enough for you, your body shifts toward burning more fat. As that happens, ketone levels may rise, giving you a measurable signal.
